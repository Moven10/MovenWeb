Minutes In Motion

Create Club

Create the Crew. Set the pace.

Start your own club and shape the culture around how you move. Set the goals, create the challenges, bring people together, and build consistent momentum week after week. Whether it’s a small local crew or a growing global community, you control the pace, the purpose, and the progress. Dont let the pace drop, create your club and Move now.

Build something bigger than yourself.

Create your own club, invite members, and set shared goals that bring people together. From small local groups to global communities, you have the tools to inspire and lead movement wherever you are.

Create something for everyone.

Whether your community is built around walking, running, cycling, or competing, Moven gives you the freedom to design experiences that connect people of all skill levels. Lead with purpose, build momentum, and make exercise accessible for everyone who wants to be part of it.

Club Events

Move your people not just yourself

Organize local meetups, create global events, and build shared experiences that unite your members. Turn everyday exercise into meaningful events, strengthen your community, and inspire others to train together with purpose.

Create events that bring your entire community together

Join Events

Discover and join events designed to bring movement to life, from competitive challenges to community-driven milestones. Track progress in real time, compete alongside others, and be part of something bigger than a single session. Built for performance and shared energy.

Create Events

Design your own event and set the challenge your way — choose the distance, define the rules, and invite others to take part. Monitor participation live, track collective results, and shape an experience that brings people together. Built for leadership and lasting impact.

Move Together

Experience movement as a shared effort, where every session contributes to collective progress. See how others are performing, stay aligned with group goals, and build consistency side by side. Built for connection and sustained momentum.

Where Every Run Connects

Every run contributes to more than just distance — it builds improvement. Training alongside others increases consistency, sharpens pace, and raises your standard with every session. Shared progress keeps you accountable, motivated, and pushing further than you would alone. When every run connects, performance compounds.

Not Finding The Right Event? Create It!

Design an event that reflects how you want to move. Choose the terrain — road, trail, track or mixed routes — set the distance, define the pace, and customise the challenge. When the right event doesn’t exist, build it — and lead the movement your way.
