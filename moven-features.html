We track what you can't.

Track heart rate, calories, weather, path, speed and more — turning every session into measurable progress.

Every Move, Mapped.

Your path is captured in real time on our custom-built maps, delivering highly accurate tracking across every run, walk, and ride. From streets to trails, every turn, pace change, and distance is recorded with precision — so you always see exactly how you moved.

Built for the Water.

Water demands precision. Traditional GPS struggles in the pool and open water, often delivering inconsistent and inaccurate results. That’s why Moven takes a different approach. Instead of relying on GPS alone, we use advanced motion and stroke detection to measure distance, pace, and performance with greater accuracy — giving you metrics you can trust, every session.

Always Connected.

Currently optimized for Apple Watch, Moven delivers industry-leading accuracy across every session. Support for Garmin and additional devices is in progress, expanding how you connect and move.

Every Step. Every Rise.

Track elevation gain and loss with precision, capturing every climb, descent, and change in terrain as you move. Combined with highly accurate step tracking, every effort is measured clearly and consistently — giving you a true picture of distance, intensity, and progress across every session.

Designed to Move.

Moven Gear transforms every session into something personal, making the way you move feel unique, expressive, and different every time. With fully customizable gear coming soon, your movement won’t just be tracked — it will reflect your style, your identity, and how you choose to move.
