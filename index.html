Move and track with style and certainty.

Precision for Every Sport

Every sport moves differently — and your data should reflect that. Tracking adapts to each activity, measuring performance the way the sport actually works. A swim session captures distance differently than a track run, delivering clearer insights and more accurate progress across every way you move.

Built Around Real Conditions

Check real-time conditions before you move to stay safe, plan smarter, and understand the difficulty of your session. Weather is automatically captured during every activity and saved with your workout, so you can review conditions alongside your performance — giving deeper context to how you trained and how you felt.

Intelligent Swim Tracking

Swim sessions are currently tracked through Apple Watch, with support for more Bluetooth-enabled devices coming soon. Movement data is analysed using sensor-based stroke detection, allowing each stroke and lap to be measured accurately in real time. Moven supports four swim types — 25m pool, 50m pool, 100m pool, and open water — and calculates average pace based on your selected lap distance, delivering clear insight into performance across every session.

Move, Personalised

Moven Gear makes every session feel personal and uniquely yours. If no sport is selected, sessions default to Move, designed for everyday movement and natural activity. For deeper performance detail and a more immersive experience, simply equip gear and move with it. Custom gear creation is coming soon, with creators and brands also building exclusive gear behind the scenes.

Performance, Fully Captured

On iOS, Moven tracks steps, pace, distance, route, speed, speed zones, splits, elevation, time, weather, fastest speed, and fastest split — delivering a complete picture of every session. When paired with Apple Watch, heart rate, heart rate zones, and calories burned are added for deeper insight. Not every session shows the same metrics — data is intelligently tailored to your activity type. With more Bluetooth-enabled devices coming soon, performance insight will continue to expand and improve.

Move Together

Connect with friends, follow their sessions, and stay inspired by the way they move. Share your own sessions across social platforms or keep them private when you choose — giving you full control over how and when you share your movement.

Offline, Handled

If service drops during a session, your activity is safely stored on your device so nothing is lost. Once you're back in service, you can review the session and choose to save it or delete it — giving you full control and peace of mind wherever you move.
